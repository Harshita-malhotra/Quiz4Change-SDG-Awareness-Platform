Sustainable Development Goal 2

Zero Hunger

Overview

SDG 2 aims to end hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture by 2030. It focuses on ensuring that everyone has access to sufficient and nutritious food.

Key Targets

  • End all forms of hunger and malnutrition by 2025.
  • Ensure sustainable food production systems.
  • Maintain the genetic diversity of seeds, cultivated plants, and farmed animals.

Global Statistics

As of 2021:

  • Approximately 811 million people are undernourished.
  • 1 in 3 people suffer from malnutrition.
  • About 690 million people are hungry.
