Sustainable Development Goal 7

Affordable and Clean Energy

Overview

SDG 7 aims to ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for all. Energy is a crucial enabler of sustainable development and is essential for poverty alleviation, economic growth, and environmental sustainability.

Key Targets

  • Ensure universal access to affordable electricity for all by 2030.
  • Increase substantially the share of renewable energy in the global energy mix by 2030.
  • Enhance international cooperation to facilitate access to clean energy research and technology.

Global Statistics

As of 2021:

  • Approximately 759 million people worldwide still lack access to electricity.
  • Renewable energy sources accounted for 29% of global electricity generation in 2020.
  • Over 2.8 billion people rely on wood, charcoal, coal, or kerosene for cooking and heating.
