Sustainable Development Goal 1: No Poverty

Overview

The first Sustainable Development Goal aims to end poverty in all its forms everywhere. Poverty is a major barrier to sustainable development, affecting millions of people around the world.

Why It Matters

Poverty affects people’s ability to live healthy lives, obtain education, and access basic services. Achieving this goal will ensure that everyone can enjoy a decent standard of living.

Goals and Targets

  • End extreme poverty for all people everywhere.
  • Reduce by half the number of people living in poverty in all its dimensions.
  • Implement nationally appropriate social protection systems and measures.
  • Ensure equal access to resources and basic services.

Call to Action

Join the global movement to end poverty. Your actions can make a difference. Whether through volunteering, advocacy, or donations, every effort counts!
