Sustainable Development Goal 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure

Overview

The ninth Sustainable Development Goal focuses on building resilient infrastructure, promoting inclusive and sustainable industrialization, and fostering innovation. SDG 9 plays a critical role in transforming economies and advancing sustainable development.

Why It Matters

Industry, innovation, and infrastructure are the backbone of economic growth and development. Sustainable industrialization and innovation foster inclusive economies, creating jobs, enhancing productivity, and reducing inequalities, thus improving overall well-being.

Goals and Targets

  • Develop quality, reliable, sustainable, and resilient infrastructure to support economic development.
  • Promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization, including increasing the share of industry in employment and GDP.
  • Increase the access of small-scale industries and businesses to financial services, including through affordable loans.
  • Enhance scientific research, upgrade technological capabilities, and foster innovation.
  • Improve access to information and communications technology and strive to provide universal and affordable internet access.

Call to Action

Help drive innovation and industrialization for a sustainable future. Support projects, policies, and initiatives that foster innovation, develop infrastructure, and promote economic development. Together, we can create the industries and infrastructure of tomorrow!
