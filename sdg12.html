Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

Overview

Sustainable Development Goal 12 seeks to ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns. This goal promotes the efficient use of resources, reduction of waste, and sustainable practices across industries. It aims to encourage businesses, governments, and individuals to adopt practices that contribute to environmental sustainability and social well-being.

Why It Matters

Our current consumption and production patterns are putting immense pressure on the planet's resources and ecosystems. Overconsumption and inefficient use of resources contribute to environmental degradation, climate change, and loss of biodiversity. Achieving SDG 12 is essential for ensuring a more sustainable and equitable future for all.

Goals and Targets

  • Implement the 10-year framework of programs on sustainable consumption and production patterns.
  • Reduce food waste at the retail and consumer levels, as well as food losses along production and supply chains.
  • Ensure that all people have the relevant information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in harmony with nature.
  • Promote the use of environmentally sound technologies and industrial processes to reduce the environmental impact of production.
  • Encourage companies to adopt sustainable practices and integrate sustainability information into their reporting cycle.

Call to Action

Be a part of the movement to foster sustainable consumption and production. Choose products that are eco-friendly, reduce waste, and advocate for sustainable practices in your community. Small actions from individuals and businesses can lead to big changes in the way we interact with the environment.
