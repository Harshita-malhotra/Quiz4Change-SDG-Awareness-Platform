Sustainable Development Goal 5

Gender Equality

Overview

SDG 5 aims to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls. Gender equality is not only a fundamental human right but a necessary foundation for a peaceful, prosperous, and sustainable world.

Key Targets

  • End all forms of discrimination against all women and girls everywhere.
  • Eliminate all forms of violence against women and girls in public and private spheres.
  • Ensure women's full and effective participation and equal opportunities for leadership at all levels of decision-making.

Global Statistics

As of 2021:

  • Approximately 1 in 3 women worldwide have experienced physical or sexual violence.
  • Women hold only 26.1% of seats in national parliaments.
  • Nearly 750 million women and girls alive today were married before their 18th birthday.
