Sustainable Development Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals

Overview

Sustainable Development Goal 17 emphasizes the need for global partnerships to drive the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It stresses the importance of collaboration among governments, the private sector, civil society, and other stakeholders to mobilize the resources and expertise required to tackle global challenges and achieve sustainable development for all.

Why It Matters

Partnerships are essential to the successful implementation of the SDGs. No single entity can achieve these goals alone. Strong, inclusive partnerships provide the means to share knowledge, technologies, and financial resources, and ensure the coordinated efforts necessary to address global challenges such as poverty, inequality, climate change, and more. By working together, we can leverage diverse resources and expertise to drive meaningful change.

Goals and Targets

  • Strengthen the means of implementation and revitalize the global partnership for sustainable development.
  • Mobilize financial resources to support the implementation of the SDGs.
  • Enhance global support for the achievement of the SDGs through technology transfer and capacity-building.
  • Promote multi-stakeholder partnerships to support the SDGs at all levels.
  • Ensure effective and inclusive global partnerships, fostering transparency, accountability, and shared responsibility.

Call to Action

We all have a role to play in strengthening global partnerships. Whether you are part of the private sector, a government, a non-governmental organization, or an individual, your actions can contribute to achieving the SDGs. Support collaboration, share knowledge and resources, and work together towards a more sustainable future for all.
