Sustainable Development Goal 10: Reduced Inequality

Overview

Sustainable Development Goal 10 focuses on reducing inequality within and among countries. This goal aims to ensure that no one is left behind, emphasizing social, economic, and political inclusion for all, regardless of age, sex, disability, race, ethnicity, origin, or economic or other status.

Why It Matters

Reducing inequality is crucial for promoting social stability, sustainable development, and ensuring equitable access to opportunities. Inequalities contribute to poverty, limited access to education, healthcare, and economic resources, creating barriers for individuals to thrive.

Goals and Targets

  • Reduce income inequality within and among countries.
  • Ensure equal opportunity and reduce inequalities of outcome, including by eliminating discriminatory laws, policies, and practices.
  • Empower and promote the social, economic, and political inclusion of all, particularly the most vulnerable groups, such as migrants, people with disabilities, and those marginalized by race or gender.
  • Ensure equal access to economic resources, education, healthcare, and opportunities for all.
  • Adopt policies that progressively achieve greater equality by addressing the root causes of inequality.

Call to Action

Join the global effort to reduce inequality by supporting inclusive policies, advocating for social justice, and ensuring that the benefits of growth and development are shared by all. Together, we can create a world where everyone has equal opportunities to succeed!
