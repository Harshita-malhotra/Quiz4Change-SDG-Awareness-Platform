Sustainable Development Goal 6

Clean Water and Sanitation

Overview

SDG 6 aims to ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all. Water is essential for life, health, and development, and access to clean water and sanitation is a fundamental human right.

Key Targets

  • Achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all by 2030.
  • Ensure access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene facilities for all.
  • Protect and restore water-related ecosystems, such as rivers, wetlands, and lakes.

Global Statistics

As of 2021:

  • 2.2 billion people lack access to safely managed drinking water services.
  • More than 4.2 billion people lack safely managed sanitation services.
  • Approximately 1 million people die each year from water-related diseases.
