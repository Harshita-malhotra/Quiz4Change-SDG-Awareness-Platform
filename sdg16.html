Sustainable Development Goal 16: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions

Overview

Sustainable Development Goal 16 promotes the establishment of peaceful, just, and inclusive societies. It focuses on providing access to justice for all, building accountable and transparent institutions, and ensuring that human rights are respected and upheld. Achieving this goal is fundamental to the overall success of sustainable development.

Why It Matters

Peace, justice, and strong institutions are at the core of achieving all other sustainable development goals. Without peace, effective governance, and access to justice, economic and social progress will remain out of reach for many. Ensuring inclusive and accountable institutions is crucial for fostering stability, reducing inequality, and protecting human rights.

Goals and Targets

  • Significantly reduce all forms of violence and related death rates everywhere.
  • Ensure equal access to justice for all and build effective, accountable, and inclusive institutions at all levels.
  • Promote and enforce non-discriminatory laws and policies for sustainable development.
  • Ensure responsive, inclusive, participatory, and representative decision-making at all levels.
